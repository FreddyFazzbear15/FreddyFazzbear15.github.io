How to Connect Printful to Etsy and Shopify

Published October 26, 2026. Facts checked October 26, 2026.

Short answer: Printful connects to Etsy and Shopify separately. You link your Etsy shop from the Printful Dashboard, and you link your Shopify store by installing the Printful app inside Shopify. After that, each shop is its own store in Printful, with its own products, prices and shipping. The setup takes minutes. Keeping the two shops matching afterwards is the part that takes planning, and this guide covers both.

Before you start

  • A Printful account. Free to create.
  • An open Etsy shop, not just a buyer account. Printful's help center says connecting a personal account gives a "Shop not found" error, and only active seller shops can connect.
  • A Shopify store on a paid plan before any trial ends. Printful notes that if a Shopify trial expires without a paid plan, the store is disconnected from Printful.
  • A naming plan for your variants. Decide now how you write colors and sizes ("Heather Grey", "XL"). You'll want them identical in both shops.

Step 1: Connect your Etsy shop to Printful

  1. In your Printful Dashboard, go to Stores → Choose platform → Etsy.
  2. Click Connect to Etsy.
  3. Allow access when Etsy asks. If no Etsy tab opens, Printful suggests turning off pop-up blockers, clearing cache and cookies, or trying another browser.

If the connection fails, check whether the shop is already connected to a different Printful account. According to Printful, you have to remove it from that account yourself under Settings → Store settings → General → Remove store.

Step 2: Connect your Shopify store to Printful

  1. In your Shopify Admin, open Apps and add the Printful app. You can also start from the Printful Dashboard: Stores → Shopify → Connect.
  2. Log in to Printful inside the embedded app in Shopify.
  3. Check that you now see the Printful Dashboard inside Shopify. If you still see a sign-in screen, Printful says the connection isn't complete. Sign in again.

Selling outside your home country? Printful's guide points out that you need to set up Markets in Shopify, or customers in some countries can't check out.

Step 3: Create a product once, then reuse it

This is the step that decides whether your two shops stay matched. Printful doesn't copy a product from one store to another directly. Instead:

  1. Design the product in Printful's Design Maker for one store, say Etsy.
  2. Save it as a product template. Printful's Shopify guide recommends templates for reusing a design across stores and platforms.
  3. Open your other store in Printful and create the product from that template.

Both listings now start from the same colors, sizes and print files. Edit the title, description and tags for each platform's audience, but leave the variants alone.

Already have products in Shopify? Printful has an "Import not synced products from Shopify" toggle under your store, and a Sync product option to match each one to a Printful product and design.

Step 4: Publish on Etsy (drafts first)

Products Printful pushes to Etsy arrive as drafts, not live listings. To publish:

  1. In Etsy, go to Shop Manager → Listings → Drafts.
  2. Open the listing and confirm Printful is listed as your production partner.
  3. Check the shipping settings, then click Publish.

Printful's help center notes that Etsy charges a USD 0.20 fee for each published listing, and a fee on each sale. Price with both in mind.

Step 5: Get shipping right in each shop

Shipping works differently on the two platforms, and this is where sellers running both shops get caught.

Etsy: flat-rate profiles only

  • Printful automatically creates flat-rate shipping profiles and processing profiles (typically 3 to 5 business days) in Etsy when you publish.
  • Don't edit those profiles. Printful overwrites manual changes on the next product update or sync. To customize, duplicate the Printful profile in Etsy, rename it, change the copy and assign your listings to it.
  • Etsy doesn't support live shipping rates from Printful, so the flat rates need to cover real costs.
  • For products with size-based shipping, such as posters, Printful says the profile uses the highest rate across the sizes you enable.
  • Printful also advises setting your Etsy order processing schedule to Monday to Friday under Shop Manager → Settings → Delivery settings.

Shopify: live rates are an option

Shopify can show Printful's live shipping rates at checkout, which Etsy can't. That means the same shirt can show a different shipping price in each shop. That's normal, but decide on purpose whether you'll absorb the difference in the item price or let it show.

Traps that break listings when you run both shops

  • Etsy's 100-variant cap. Printful's help center says Etsy listings max out at 100 variants. A shirt with 12 colors and 9 sizes is 108, so it fails on Etsy while working fine on Shopify. Trim colors on both shops so the variants match.
  • Tags over 20 characters. "Product was not added! Unknown Etsy client exception" is, per Printful, almost always a tag longer than Etsy's 20-character limit. Check tags you copied over from Shopify or another tool before you publish.
  • The 20-image limit. If an Etsy listing already has 20 images, Printful can't push a new mockup to it.
  • A changed Etsy password. Printful notes that changing your Etsy password revokes API tokens. If orders stop importing after a password change, click Reconnect on the store in Printful. Products and settings aren't affected.
  • Prices drift. Retail prices are set per store. Raise a price in one and the other stays where it was. Keep a simple list of your prices in both shops and update them together.

A 10-minute checklist after setup

  1. Both stores show as Active under Stores in Printful.
  2. Every product in both shops was created from a shared template.
  3. Variant names and counts match between shops, and no Etsy listing exceeds 100 variants.
  4. No tag is longer than 20 characters.
  5. Etsy listings use Printful's shipping profile or your own copy of it, not an edited original.
  6. Shopify Markets are set up for every country you ship to.
  7. Your Shopify plan is paid, not a trial about to end.
  8. You have one list of SKUs and retail prices covering both shops.

Keeping both shops in step

Printful handles printing and fulfillment for each shop. What it doesn't do is keep your Etsy listing and your Shopify product identical once you start editing them. That's the problem we're working on.
