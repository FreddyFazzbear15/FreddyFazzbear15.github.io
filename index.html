The problem

Two shops shouldn't mean twice the work

Running an Etsy shop and a Shopify store side by side is a good way to reach more buyers. Keeping them in sync by hand is not.

Overselling

Your last one-of-a-kind piece sells on Etsy at 2am. By morning someone has bought it again on Shopify, and now you're writing an apology and issuing a refund.

Double work

Every restock, price change and new colourway gets typed in twice. Miss one and the two shops quietly drift apart, with no single source of truth for what you actually have.

How it will work

Three steps to keep inventory in sync

  1. Connect both shops

    Link your Etsy shop and your Shopify store. Both stay open and selling exactly as they are now.

  2. Match your products

    We suggest matches between listings and variants, even when sizes or colours are named or ordered differently on each store. You confirm or adjust them.

  3. Sell anywhere

    When something sells on either shop, stock updates on the other in real time. Shared materials count down across every listing that uses them.

FAQ

Questions sellers ask

Is this approved by Etsy?

Not yet. We're in early access. We'll only connect to Etsy through the official Etsy API, and Etsy reviews any app before it can work with other sellers' shops. We'll tell everyone on the list how that review goes. TwinStock is not endorsed or certified by Etsy, Inc.

Can I use it today?

Not yet. TwinStock is still being built. Join the early-access list and we'll email you when it's ready to try.

Do my Etsy shop and Shopify store stay as they are?

Yes. Both shops keep running and selling exactly as they do now. The goal is simply to keep inventory and listings in sync between them, so you have one single source of truth and stop doing the same job twice.

I use Printful. Will that work?

Print-on-demand with Printful is one of the first setups we're designing for, including products where size and colour variants are set up differently on each store.
